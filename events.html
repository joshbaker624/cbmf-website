The lineup

Something for everyone.

Every event raises money and awareness for families living with ALS.

ALS Walk-a-Thon

Lace up and walk together in Charlotte's honor. It's a powerful kickoff to the weekend and a chance to remember the people ALS has touched.

Kicks off the weekend

Hog Cook-Off & BBQ Plate Sale

Pitmasters compete, and everyone else eats. Grab a plate of eastern North Carolina barbecue and know it's going to a great cause.

BBQ plates for sale

Vendor Booths

Local makers, crafters and small businesses set up shop downtown. Shop local and support ALS families at the same time.

Booth space available

Half-and-Half Raffle

Buy a ticket and half the pot goes to one lucky winner. The other half goes to helping families facing ALS.

Tickets just $1
All hands on deck

Powered by volunteers

It takes a town.

From the kitchen at the Pink Hill Fire Department to the vendor booths downtown, the weekend runs on volunteers in memorial T-shirts who give their time for families they may never meet.

Whether you can smoke a hog, sell raffle tickets or just show up hungry, there's a place for you.

Looking back

Past Memorial Weekends.

2022

Where it started

  • BBQ Cook-Off and $10 plate sales
  • Co-ed softball tournament at the Albertson Ball Field
  • Half-and-half raffle drawing at Two Dogs Pizza
2023

$25,000 raised

  • The community raised $25,000 for people living with ALS
  • Proof of what a small town can do together
2024

A full weekend, Sept 27–29

  • ALS Walk-a-Thon in Pink Hill
  • Hog cook-off, BBQ sale and vendor booths on W. Walnut Street
  • Softball at the Pink Hill & Albertson fields
  • Raffle drawing at Two Dogs Pizza
