Questions

Donor FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Charlotte Baker Memorial Fund, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 88-3524209). Donations are generally tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please check with your tax advisor about your situation.

Who does my donation help?

ALS patients and their families in our local eastern North Carolina community. Funds go toward needs like equipment, home modifications and financial assistance.

Is giving online secure?

Yes. Online donations are processed by Square, a trusted payment provider. Your card information goes directly to Square and is never stored by us.

Can I give in memory or in honor of someone?

Absolutely. After you give, send us a message on Facebook with the name of the person you're honoring and we'll make sure it's recognized.

My family is facing ALS. How do we ask for help?

We're so sorry you're going through this, and we'd be honored to help. Reach out to us privately through Facebook and a member of our team will be in touch.

Charlotte Baker Memorial Fund, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Pink Hill, North Carolina, helping ALS patients and their families.
